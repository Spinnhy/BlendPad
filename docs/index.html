Built for Serious 3D Artists

Hardware SpaceMouses cost $150–$400. BlendPad turns the multi-touch display you already own into an ergonomic, zero-lag controller.

120Hz UDP Streaming

Continuous delta coordinates sent over native UDP sockets without web socket bloat. Zero perceptible latency, silky smooth 120 FPS camera rotations.

Multi-Touch Gestures

1 finger to orbit around selections. 2 fingers to pan along the viewport plane. Pinch-to-zoom with continuous precision scaling.

Context-Aware Decks

When you press Tab to enter Sculpt or Edit Mode in Blender, BlendPad automatically shifts buttons to show relevant tools, dyntopo, brushes, or topology actions.

Instant QR Quick-Pair

No IP typing required. Click "Mostra QR" in the Blender sidebar, point your camera, and connect in less than 2 seconds with automatic local token verification.

Custom Dials & Sliders

Continuous rotary dials for Brush Radius, Strength, Subsurf levels, Timeline Scrubbing, and Transform axes without reaching for your keyboard.

8 Languages Supported

Fully localized in English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Automatically matches your system language.

Context-Aware Workflow Decks

BlendPad listens to Blender's active mode in real time and automatically transforms your phone screen.

Sculpt Deck

Fast brush switching, continuous radius & strength rotary dials, Remesh triggers, and symmetry locks right under your thumb.

Setup in 3 Easy Steps

Get connected to your Blender viewport in under 60 seconds.

01

Install Blender Add-on

Download blendpad_blender.zip. In Blender, go to Edit > Preferences > Add-ons, click Install from Disk, and enable BlendPad Direct.

Preferences > Add-ons > Install
02

Start BlendPad Server

Press N in your 3D Viewport to open the sidebar. Click the BlendPad tab and hit Avvia Server. The QR code appears automatically.

View3D > Sidebar (N) > BlendPad
03

Scan & Navigate

Open the BlendPad mobile app on your Android smartphone or tablet. Tap Scan QR, point at your monitor, and start navigating instantly!
